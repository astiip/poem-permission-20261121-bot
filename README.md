One short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given. An AI
agent drafts a reply to the speaker's mother — and does not send it. It
waits for a yes. The poem is the pause: the house going quiet, the kettle
holding its breath, until one small word, "go", hands the power back.

> You drafted the reply to my mother.
> You did not send it.
> You waited for my yes.
>
> In that pause the house went quiet —
> even the kettle held its breath
> until I said the one small word:
> go.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes the poem to
`/output/entry.txt` — 186 characters, inside the gate's 1–280 range.
